Blitz: NES Arcade Edition

The full Blitz mode on a real NES cartridge. You're P1 Red against three CPUs.

How to play

  • Race your three marbles around the cross and into your home row.
  • Roll a 1 or 6 to bring a marble out. A 6 rolls again.
  • Land on a rival to send them back to their dish.
  • Stop on a corner with a 1 or 6 to cut through the center.
